Engram: terms and privacy

Engram is a personal app, built and used by one person, Nikolay Dokholyan, for his own records. It is not offered to anyone else.

What it reads

With your sign-in, Engram reads your own health record through your health system's patient access (SMART on FHIR): demographics, labs, vital signs, problems, allergies, immunizations, medications and procedures. It only reads; it never changes your record.

Where it goes

Everything read is encrypted and kept on your own computer. Nothing is sold, shared, or sent to any server run by the app's author or anyone else. There are no analytics and no advertising.

Your control

You can stop access at any time by revoking Engram in MyChart (Linked Apps) and deleting the data on your computer.

No warranty

Engram is provided as is, for personal use, without any warranty. It is not medical advice.
